Welcome to Lugore Prison Farm - Gulu, Uganda

Our Main Gate - Lugore Prison Farm

We train, farm and rehabilitate through hard work and discipline. Lugore Prison Farm is located 33km north of Gulu City in Lugore Parish, Owalo Sub-county, Aswa County, Gulu District, established in 1950 on 2418 acres of land.

Lugore is Uganda's model prison farm for maize, sunflower, mangoes, oranges, eucalyptus and vegetables - farming for rehabilitation, food security and discipline.

🎯 VISION, MISSION & OBJECTIVES

👁️ VISION OF THE FARM

A centre of excellence in providing human right based correctional services in Africa

🌟 MISSION OF THE FARM

To provide safe, secure and humane custody of offenders while placing human rights at the centre of their correctional programs

✅ OBJECTIVES OF THE FARM

✔️ To use farming or production as rehabilitation programs of offenders

✔️ To supplement on the food needs of prisoners while in custody

📖 BACKGROUND OF THE FARM

The establishment of UGPFL was in 1950 and occupies 2418 acres of land. UGPFL is located in Lugore Parish, Owalo Sub-county, Aswa County and Gulu District.

It is 33Km north of Gulu city along road heading to Lamwo District. On these 2418 acres of land, there are various activities that take place and these include crop production, livestock management and afforestation.

Under afforestation, the clonal hybrid eucalyptus on 120 acres, under crop production, the major crops grown are maize and sun flower on 1300 acres, under orchard establishment 75 acres are occupied where by 55 acres are occupied by grafted mangoes and 20 acres are occupied by budded oranges. More so, are short fruit trees like jack fruit, avocado, bananas among others under establishment and well cover ten additional acres.

The Farm also undertakes vegetable growing covering ten acres. This is made for the inmate nutritional needs and demand.

The following are the vegetables planted; onions, eggplants, okra, tomatoes, sukuma wiki and cabbage etc.

📰 NEWS - OUR ACHIEVEMENTS - 2026 KACOKE MADIT AWARDS

📅 Posted on: 13 November 2026

UGANDA PRISONS SERVICE - OUTSTANDING PERFORMANCE AWARDS

Lugore Prison Farm was recognised among the best-performing prison farms in Uganda at the 2026 Senior Officers' Annual Management Meeting - Kacoke Madit.

🌽 1. MAIZE GRAIN PRODUCTION - RUNNER-UP IN UGANDA

Lugore was recognised as Runner-Up for Best Maize Grain Production in Uganda. Uganda Government Prisons Farm Ibuga emerged as the best performer.

🐄 2. LIVESTOCK PRODUCTION - RUNNER-UP IN UGANDA

Lugore was recognised as Runner-Up for Best Livestock Production in Uganda. Uganda Prisons Fort Portal Main emerged as the best-performing station.

🚀 3. COMMERCIAL MAIZE EXPANSION

Lugore was recognised for expanding its commercial maize production from an initial target of 200 acres to 1,200 acres, alongside its contribution to livestock development, afforestation and agricultural skills training.

Awards presented by the Commissioner General of Prisons, Canon Dr. Johnson O. R. Byabashaija, at the 2026 Senior Officers' Annual Management Meeting - Kacoke Madit.
